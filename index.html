Case Studies

Production Platforms

Solar Rooftop NOC Portal

Tata Power • Real-Time Dashboard

The Story: Tata Power needed a way to monitor rooftop solar panels across the country without sending technicians out for every manual check. I built the front-end operations dashboard (NOC) that shows live power generation, offline statuses, and alerts. It gained major traction, even being heavily used by communities in Odisha to locate and monitor solar panels.

What I learned & can build now: I mastered parsing streams of live data and creating highly scannable analytics dashboards. I learned how to handle polling, real-time alerts, and data visualization tools, effectively improving our visual clarity by 25%.

EZ Charge Web App

Tata Power • Customer-Facing EV App

The Story: As EV adoption surged, customers needed a seamless way to locate charging stations, initiate a charge, and pay securely from their phones. I built the entire customer-facing front-end for this application. The app rapidly scaled, hitting over 10,000 active users within its first six months.

What I learned & can build now: This project taught me advanced state management using Redux across multiple screens (e.g., tracking an active charging session while navigating). I also learned how to properly implement secure payment gateway integrations and manage REST/GraphQL data fetching.

Unified Customer Portal

Tata Power • Self-Service Hub

The Story: Customers were tired of navigating multiple systems or calling support to pay a bill, submit a meter reading, or raise a service ticket. I consolidated all billing, usage, and device data into a single, unified React portal.

What I learned & can build now: I learned how to architect reusable component libraries. I built highly reusable form, card, and modal APIs that other developers could easily consume, drastically cutting development time. Moving these manual processes online reduced support inquiries by 15%.
